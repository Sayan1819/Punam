About Us

Painters who turn up on time.

ColourCraft Painters started in 2018 with two painters and a simple promise: finish when we say we will, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

Today a crew of 24 handles homes, shops and offices across Kolkata. Every site gets a supervisor, a written schedule and a final walk-through with you.

We use branded paints only, mix colours by code so touch-ups match later, and cover every floor and fitting before we open a tin.

What we promise

On-time delivery

If we finish late because of us, you get 5% off the final bill for each day.

Neat work

Sharp edges, masked fittings and no paint on your floors or furniture.

Clear pricing

The quote lists area, coats and paint brand. Nothing is added later without your approval.
